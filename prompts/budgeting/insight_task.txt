Review this customer's budgets for the month and write a personalised spending insight of 100 to 160 words.

Cover, in this order:
1. One sentence on overall position: total spent against total budgeted.
2. The categories that are over budget, with the amount over for each.
3. The categories with room left that could absorb the overspend.
4. One or two specific adjustments to make for the rest of the month.

If nothing is over budget, say so plainly and point out where the customer is doing well.
